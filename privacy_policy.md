# Privacy Policy for Valuation Toolkit

**Last updated: March 22, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Valuation Toolkit ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information Collection and Sharing with Third-Party AI

We do not collect personal data directly to our own servers. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect and process information you provide to deliver the App's core functionality.

**Data Shared with Third-Party AI Services (Google Gemini):**
In order to provide our AI Analyst forecasting and chart analysis features, the App requires your explicit consent to share specific data with Google's Gemini AI API. 
*   **What Data is Sent:** The stock charts (images) you explicitly select to upload and the stock ticker symbols you enter in the AI Analyst screen.
*   **Who it is Sent to:** Google LLC (Google Gemini AI service).
*   **How it is Used:** This data is processed exclusively in real-time to generate the financial analysis and forecasts displayed to you within the App. 
*   **Data Protection:** Your data is not stored by us, nor is it used by us to train any artificial intelligence models. We confirm that Google provides a level of protection for this data that is equal to or greater than our own standards, in accordance with their API data privacy policies.

### Other Third-Party Services
*   **Google Firebase Authentication**: We use Firebase to authenticate users. This service may collect your email address or Apple ID identifier solely for the purpose of managing your account and securing your data.
*   **Yahoo Finance API**: We use Yahoo Finance to fetch stock market data. Your search queries (stock symbols) are sent to this service to retrieve data.

## 2. How We Use Information

*   **Authentication**: To verify your identity and allow you to save your preferences or portfolios securely.
*   **Service Provision**: To provide the core features of the app (e.g., AI-powered stock analysis, data retrieval).
*   **Improvements**: We may use aggregated, anonymized data (like crash reports via Apple) to improve the stability of the App.

## 3. Data Retention

We do not store your personal data on our own servers. Authentication data is managed by Google Firebase. You can delete your account at any time within the app (if applicable) or by contacting us, which will remove your credentials from the authentication provider.

## 4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
